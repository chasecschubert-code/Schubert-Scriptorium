The Curator’s Prism is our premium glass-acrylic display. Unlike the wood-base versions, the Prism is a heavy, solid block of glass-acrylic that stands independently. It creates a stunning "floating" effect, where the light catches the edges of the papyrus from all 360 degrees. It is modern, sleek, and minimalist, designed to let the ancient fragment speak for itself without the visual weight of a base.
