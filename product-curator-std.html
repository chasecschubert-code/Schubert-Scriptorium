Curator's Prism

$65

The Curator’s Prism is our premium glass-acrylic display. Unlike the Gallery Editions, the Prism is a heavier block made from two opposing glass-acrylic frames that are secured with small embedded magnets on each corner. It creates a stunning "floating" effect, where the light catches the edges of the papyrus from all sides. It is modern, sleek, and minimalist, designed to let the ancient fragment speak for itself without the visual weight of a base.

Includes custom translation card and microfiber cleaning cloth.
