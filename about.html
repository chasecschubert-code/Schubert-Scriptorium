A Witness to the Truth

My fascination with biblical manuscripts isn't just about history—it’s about the faithfulness of God. I founded Schubert Scriptorium to bridge the gap between a photo in a textbook and the physical reality of history. I want people to be able to hold a powerful and tangible echo of the past and be reminded of the generations of Christians who faithfully transcribed the Scriptures so that we might have them today.

Why P52?

Discovered in Egypt and dating to the early 2nd century, Papyrus 52 is a testimony to how quickly the Gospel of John was being copied and circulated within a single generation of the Apostles. The fragment captures a pivotal moment: Jesus’ trial before Pilate.

On the verso (back) of this fragment, we find the very words of Jesus declaring His mission: "...to bear witness to the truth." It is a profound providence that this specific fragment was preserved—recording the conversation that leads directly to Pilate’s famous question: "What is truth?"
